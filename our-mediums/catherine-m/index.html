About Catherine M.

Catherine has practised mediumship for more than twenty years, and she co-founded Free Medium Reading with Robert L. because she kept meeting people who needed a quiet, respectful space to ask about a loss and could not justify the cost of a private session. Her working style is visual: she describes the images, scenes and small objects that come to her, then leaves it to you to decide whether they mean something.

Sitters often say the first few minutes are the hardest, which is why Catherine begins slowly. She will explain how she works, what she can and cannot promise, and invite you to say as much or as little about your loved one as you like. She does not ask for a full life story and does not rush towards a conclusion. If an impression does not land, she says so and moves on rather than stretching it.

What she is known for is the detail: a way of tilting the head, a coat that hung by the door, a kitchen where something was always baking. She describes these as impressions she receives, and she is careful with the language. She does not claim to speak with the dead on command or to deliver guaranteed messages. Mediumship is an experience many people find comforting and meaningful, and it remains a belief-based practice rather than something that can be scientifically verified.

Her sessions suit people who are earlier in grief and want a gentle first step, as well as those who have been carrying something unsaid for years. If you are sceptical, that is welcome too. Catherine would rather you ask direct questions than nod along.

Outside readings, she gardens, keeps a handwritten journal of every question she has been asked, and volunteers with a local bereavement circle in New England. A reading with Catherine is an invitation to sit with your memories in good company, not a replacement for grief counselling, medical care or professional support.

Specialties

Clairvoyance

Visual impressions such as a face, a favourite coat or a place that mattered, shared as impressions rather than proof.

Spirit communication

A patient, unhurried attempt to sense what a loved one might want to say, with room for silence.

Grief support

Sessions paced around your loss, with no expectation that you feel anything in particular.

Questions Catherine often works with

  • I lost my mother last spring and keep dreaming about her kitchen. Is there anything in that for me?
  • What might my grandfather want me to know about the decision I am facing?
  • I never got to say goodbye. How do I begin to make peace with that?

A reading is offered for comfort and reflection. It cannot guarantee contact with anyone who has died, and it is not medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice. If you are in crisis, please contact a local professional or crisis line.

How a session with Catherine unfolds

  1. Settle in

    A short, calm introduction so you know exactly what will happen and can stop at any time.

  2. Receive impressions

    Catherine describes images and details she senses and checks them with you, one at a time.

  3. Sort what fits

    You decide what resonates. Anything that does not fit is set aside without argument.

  4. Close gently

    She gathers the themes, offers a few thoughts for reflection and suggests support options if useful.

Catherine: frequently asked questions

Does Catherine promise that my loved one will come through?

No. She describes the impressions she receives and is clear that contact cannot be guaranteed. Some sessions are quiet, and she will tell you if that happens.

Do I need to believe in mediumship before a session?

No. Open curiosity is enough, and honest scepticism is welcome.

Is a session a replacement for grief counselling?

No. Mediumship can be a comfort for some people, but it is not therapy or medical advice. If you are struggling, please contact a qualified professional or a crisis line.
